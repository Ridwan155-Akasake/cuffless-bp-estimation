# Online PPG-BP

Files (now in `_archive/datasets/`): `ppg-bp_data_online.csv`, `ppg_bp_data_cleaned.csv`,
`ppg_bp_data_cleaned(1).csv`, `ppg_data_only.csv`, `extracted_features_ppg_online.csv`,
`Generated_PPG_Blood_Pressure_Dataset.csv`.

## What

Public / synthetic PPG-BP datasets pulled in during early experimentation.

## Why It's Archived

- `ppg_data_only.csv` had no BP labels.
- The cleaned online sets did not match this project's sensor / sampling setup.
- The "Generated" dataset is synthetic and was not used in the final training.
