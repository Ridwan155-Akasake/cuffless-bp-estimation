# Multi-output combined experiments

Files (now in `_archive/models/`):
- `multi_model_combined_rf.pkl`
- `multi_model_combined_catboost.pkl`
- `multi_model_combined_lgbm.pkl`
- `multi_model_combined_adaboost.pkl`
- `multi_model_combined_ridge.pkl`

## What

Multi-output regressors — one model predicts both systolic and diastolic — across five
algorithm families. Generated by `models/multi_target_train.py`.

## Trained On

- [[all_features_dataset]].

## Why They're Archived

The Random Forest variant of this run is what eventually became
[[random_forest_tuned_model]] (after further tuning); the CatBoost variant lives on as
[[best_catboost_model]] (also tuned). The remaining LightGBM / AdaBoost / Ridge checkpoints
underperformed and were dropped.
